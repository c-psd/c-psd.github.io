Nordic Process Science Days

January 2027/Technical University of Denmark/Kgs. Lyngby

Processes are everywhere in our everyday life and work. However, they are sometimes perceived as a hindrance instead of something that can be understood, shaped, and used for better digital solutions. The Nordic Process Science Days aim to bring together practitioners and academics from the Nordics, to discuss the latest challenges in managing processes. We hope to build a strong network that works together on solving modern day challenges.

Call for Contributions

We invite practitioners and academics from across the Nordics to shape the programme of the Nordic Process Science Days 2027. We welcome contributions — talks, experience reports, tool demonstrations, and interactive sessions — that explore how processes can be understood, shaped, and used for better digital solutions.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Process modelling, analysis, and mining
  • Automation and AI in process management
  • Compliance, governance, and regulation
  • Cyber security in process execution
  • Supply chain and cross-organisational processes
  • Human-centred process design and change

This is a placeholder description. Detailed submission guidelines, deadlines, and formats will be announced soon. We look forward to your ideas and to building a strong Nordic network together.
